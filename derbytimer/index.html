DerbyTimer

Roller derby penalty box timer

DerbyTimer times the penalties in a roller derby penalty box, on iPhone and iPad, made by Joël Coster.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email joelcoster@hotmail.com and I will get back to you.

Good to know

  • One iPhone or iPad hosts a session, the others join it with the 4-letter code or by scanning the QR code with the Camera app. The host can also time everything on its own.
  • Every device needs DerbyTimer, Wi-Fi turned on and Local Network access, and has to be near the host. No account or internet is needed.
  • Keep DerbyTimer open on the host: while it is in the background, the other devices can't time.
  • Can't find the session? Check the code, turn on Wi-Fi, and make sure Local Network is allowed in Settings > Apps > DerbyTimer.
  • How it works, on the start screen of the app, explains the taps: tap to start a penalty, hold to add another 30 seconds, the arrow takes that back.
  • The app has no in-app purchases, ads or accounts.
