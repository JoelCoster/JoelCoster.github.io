Privacy policy

DerbyTimer, by Joël Coster. Last updated 1 November 2026.

DerbyTimer collects nothing about you.

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing is sent to me or to anyone else, and the app does not use the internet.
  • A session runs between nearby devices over the local network. The timers, the team names and colours, and a random device ID go only to the devices that joined with the session code. The ID lets the host recognise a device that reconnects; it is not linked to you.
  • Team names and colours are saved on your device for your next session. The current session is saved too, so it can carry on after the app is closed; it is removed when the session ends, and is not restored after 12 hours.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it saved. Like everything on your device, it can be part of your own device backup.
  • If you share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may pass on anonymous crash reports. You choose this yourself, and can turn it off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.
