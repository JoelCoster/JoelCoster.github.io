Firebreak

Hold the town. Mind the wind.

Firebreak is a medieval tower defence game for iPhone and iPad, made by Joël Coster.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email joelcoster@hotmail.com and I will get back to you.

Good to know

  • Your progress is saved on your device. Deleting the game removes it; restoring a device backup brings it back.
  • Settings > Reset campaign starts the campaign over (from the title screen).
  • The game works fully offline and has no in-app purchases, ads or accounts.
