Privacy policy

Firebreak, by Joël Coster. Last updated 25 October 2026.

Firebreak collects nothing about you.

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third parties.
  • The game never uses the internet.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your device, and deleting the game removes them. Like everything on your device, they can be part of your own device backup.
  • If you share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may pass on anonymous crash reports. You choose this yourself, and can turn it off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first, with a new date.
